Research & transparency. Swarm Mind is a research-inspired proof of concept. Every map, agent and measurement is synthetic, produced by a local rule-based simulation. No large language model is involved. Results describe this simulated system only — they are not a proof of intelligence or a validation of real ant behaviour.
