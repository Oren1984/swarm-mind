# Storyboard

Planned structure and the timings actually rendered. Scene lengths follow the narration: each scene lasts as long as its sentences take to speak, plus short pauses.

## Deviations from the plan

- Scenes 2 to 5 are shorter than planned and scene 6 is longer (33 s instead of 20 s). The results needed five sentences to state the main effect, the cut, and both failure conditions without exaggeration; the demonstrations needed fewer words than budgeted.
- Total length 118.9 s, inside the 90–120 s target.
- The bottom 150 pixels of every frame are a reserved subtitle band. Footage and charts are scaled into the area above it, so subtitles never cover a metric.
- Single-world scenes are cropped to the world's panel (crop rectangle measured from the page at recording time) so the simulation fills the frame.
